Pitput sound credits
====================
Animal calls (kelev, chatul, para, barvaz, tarnegol, kivsa, sus, arye, pil, kof, tzfardea, tzipor, nachash,
dov, gamal, tarnegolet, ez, chazir, achbar, tuki, yona, dolphin, yanshuf, avaz):
  from "Animals" by Gustavo Cardoso (https://github.com/gustavocardoso/animals), MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2019 Gustavo Cardoso. Trimmed and loudness-normalized.

Vehicles, household, nature and celebration sounds (dvora, oto, otobus, rakevet, matos, masait, tractor,
mishtara, kabait, ambulans, masok, sira, ofanoa, raketa, dinozaur, tof, telefon, kadur, delet, ambatya,
balon, geshem, yam, _cheer, _claps, _magic, _xylo, _boing):
  from the Google Sound Library (https://developers.google.com/assistant/tools/sound-library),
  licensed CC BY 4.0. Trimmed and loudness-normalized.

ofanayim (bicycle bell) and shachaf (seagull call): synthesized for this app.
